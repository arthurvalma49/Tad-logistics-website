Production

How we work

Our production is in Rummu, Vasalemma parish, Harju County. We use a quilting machine, tape edge machine, side quilting machine, mattress press and other equipment, and everything is made on site.

Production is mainly project-based, and most of it is exported to Scandinavia.

TAD Logistics plays an important part in making Kamjo beds. See the range on the Kamjo Eesti OÜ website (opens in a new tab).
